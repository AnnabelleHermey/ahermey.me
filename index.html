License Plate Transformation Details

Applied affine and perspective transformations to estimate and reverse geometric distortions in images using Python and NumPy. Computed a homography matrix from point correspondences to undistort angled license plate images, simulating real-world forensic enhancement. Developed custom routines to construct and solve linear systems for both affine and perspective warping corrections.

Rinse & Ready Details

Co-invented an eco-friendly, quick, and efficient dishwashing solution for dorms and campus buildings, tailored for washing food containers. Led the design process from initial sketches to 3D modeling in SolidWorks, incorporating features like strainers, water jets, and an in-line soap dispenser. Inverted Pendulum Details

Designed and implemented a proportional-integral-derivative integral (PID) controller to stabilize an inverted pendulum mounted on a moving car, ensuring the pendulum recovered from disturbances and maintained balance by controlling the motor to drive and reverse the car. Modeled the system dynamics by deriving equations for the physical and motor systems, analyzing control loops using MATLAB, and designing a compensator circuit with root locus to achieve stability and robustness. Conducted rigorous testing, addressing challenges like jitter, drift, and hardware limitations to achieve a functional and responsive stabilization system.
